WHAT TO EXPECT

DeepDriveCleaner in Applications, and ddc in your terminal.

What does it find, and how are risks handled?
safe
Download caches. Refill when needed; selected by default.
rebuildable
Project artifacts. Default selection depends on age and rule-specific project-file checks.
review
Models, toolchains and other costly-to-restore findings. You choose each item.
user-data
Personal files are never selected automatically. Eligible chosen entries go to the Trash only.

Sample sizes, not promised savings. Classification depends on the rule and context.
