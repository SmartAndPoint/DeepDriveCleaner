The app now says where your disk space went since the earlier scan, sees Full Disk Access on macOS 27, scans by itself after an update, and repairs its scheduled scans when an update left them stopped. It also covers 0.34.1, which was tagged but never published.

> **DeepDriveCleaner deletes files.** It can remove almost anything it is told to, by you or by an agent acting for you. Use it at your own risk: nobody who made it is responsible for what it removes. Read [DISCLAIMER.md](https://github.com/SmartAndPoint/DeepDriveCleaner/blob/v0.35.0/DISCLAIMER.md).

**What grew since the earlier scan**

- **The popover names it.** After a scan, when in-use space grew by at least 20 GiB or 5 % of the disk since the scan before, a band says how much and where: "↑ 60.0 GiB more in use since yesterday 9:30 · Build outputs +50.0 GiB · 14 new · mostly in Claude Code worktrees or data", or that most of it is elsewhere in your home folder, or in folders ddc did not read or outside your home folder. It adds what you cleaned since, when that was a lot. See What Grew opens the Review window; it never ticks or cleans.
- **Review has a "What grew" entry.** Above the groups: the same rows as in their groups, the ones that are new, grew or moved, with a Change column and their own order. A tick there is the same tick in the group, and Clean N items… stays the only way to the sheet. Below: growth elsewhere in your home folder, what the scan did not read (with Scan Now, Settings…, Whole Disk… and, after a scan without Full Disk Access, Open Privacy Settings), and what you cleaned or what went.
- **`ddc scan` ends with the short form** after a scan that kept the one before: the three groups that grew most, and a pointer to `ddc changes`. Never with `--json`.
- **`ddc changes --json` gains `grew`**, the same rule as `ddc space`'s, so the app compares no threshold itself. Its schema stays 1.

**After an update**

- **The app scans by itself** when the update left a saved report it cannot read. At the first launch of a new build, with scheduled scans on and not paused, the scheduled scan runs at once, the same background scan as always (no privacy prompts). The popover says "Scanning after the update…" instead of "No scan yet". A Scan Now before it leaves no second scan. This release changes the scan report, so you will see it once after updating.
- **Scheduled scans are registered again after an update.** On some Macs launchd kept the registration of an old build and stopped starting the app's hourly tick, while macOS still showed it as allowed: no scheduled scan ran, and nothing said so. The app now registers its scheduled scan again when a new build starts from the same place. macOS may ask you to allow it again in System Settings → General → Login Items & Extensions.
- **"Scheduled scans are not running"**, with Fix, when two hours of awake time pass with no tick. Fix registers the scheduled scan again; if ticks still do not come, Settings → Schedule names Login Items and `ddc schedule install`. When macOS waits for your approval, the footer says so and leads to it.

**Full Disk Access on macOS 27.** The app and `ddc` said Full Disk Access was off with the switch on, and background scans skipped Desktop, Documents and Downloads: macOS 27 has no per-user TCC database to check, or refuses it. They now also check `~/Library/Safari`, which only Full Disk Access lists. macOS 26 answers as before.

**Files that cannot be removed.** A finding that holds an immutable or append-only file (`chflags uchg`, `chattr +i`, `chattr +a`) is no longer selected, planned or cleaned: it would sit in the Trash and never empty, or stop a delete halfway. The scan lists it as not selected (`immutable`), the clean skips it with the same reason and says which command shows the flag; ddc never clears a flag.

**Removal never crosses a mount.** An interrupted deletion's leftover folder is removed through the folder it was renamed in, level by level, without following a symlink and without entering another mount, bind mount or btrfs subvolume made inside it meanwhile (Linux and macOS).

**`.ddc-keep` in any letter case** (from 0.34.1). A `.DDC-KEEP` keeps its folder like `.ddc-keep`, in the scan, the dry run and the checks before each removal. `ddc keep remove` still removes only `.ddc-keep`, and now names a marker in another case that still protects the folder. Rule names still match byte for byte.

**Report schemas.** Scan 9, clean 12, journal 12, progress 10 (the `immutable` reason). Space 1 and changes 1 are unchanged; changes gains the always-written `grew`.

**Upgrading.** `brew upgrade --cask deepdrivecleaner`, the app's Check Now, the install script, or the zip below. If you upgrade with Homebrew while the app is quit, open it once so it registers its scheduled scan again.
