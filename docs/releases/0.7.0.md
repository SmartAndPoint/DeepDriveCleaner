Homebrew packages with their sizes and brew uninstall, and the Whole disk rows freed together in one sheet.

> **DeepDriveCleaner deletes files.** It can remove almost anything it is told to, by you or by an agent acting for you. Use it at your own risk: nobody who made it is responsible for what it removes. Read [DISCLAIMER.md](https://github.com/SmartAndPoint/DeepDriveCleaner/blob/v0.7.0/DISCLAIMER.md).

- **Homebrew packages.** The Homebrew row on Whole disk has **Packages…**: every installed formula with the size of its versions in the Cellar, measured on disk, and what needs it. A formula nothing needs has **Uninstall…**, which runs `brew uninstall --formula`, so a cask of the same name is never touched. Right before it runs, DeepDriveCleaner reads Homebrew again and refuses, saying why, when the formula was pinned, changed versions, gained a formula or cask that needs it, or has a service job. After it, the sheet offers the dependencies nothing else needs any more, with their own confirmation. The confirmation says what brew cannot know: programs outside Homebrew that use the formula, such as a virtualenv linked against its openssl.
- **Free the checked rows in one sheet.** Rows that can free space with their tool (simulator runtimes macOS can delete, Homebrew's cleanup, conda environments and package caches in /opt) have a checkbox. **Free N Checked…** opens one sheet with each tool's own dry run, its command and what it cannot undo, then one confirmation. If some can no longer run, the sheet says why and runs only the others ("Free 1 of 2"). Each row reports its own result.
- **For scripts.** `ddc disk brew-packages --json` lists the formulae ([`docs/brew-packages.schema.json`](https://github.com/SmartAndPoint/DeepDriveCleaner/blob/v0.7.0/docs/brew-packages.schema.json)). `ddc disk free` gains `brew-uninstall` and `brew-uninstall-orphans`, which the terminal prompt does not offer. The free list and report are now at version 3; the disk report stays at version 2. See [`docs/disk-free.schema.json`](https://github.com/SmartAndPoint/DeepDriveCleaner/blob/v0.7.0/docs/disk-free.schema.json).

**Upgrading.** Run `brew upgrade --cask deepdrivecleaner`. The upgrade quits the app. It opens again at your next login if Open at login is on, or when you open it yourself. Do not upgrade while the app is cleaning.
