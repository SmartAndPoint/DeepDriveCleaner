`ddc scan` now shows each git worktree with its project, says why it is not selected, and lists what lies inside it under it.

> **DeepDriveCleaner deletes files.** It can remove almost anything it is told to, by you or by an agent acting for you. Use it at your own risk: nobody who made it is responsible for what it removes. Read [DISCLAIMER.md](https://github.com/SmartAndPoint/DeepDriveCleaner/blob/v0.26.0/DISCLAIMER.md).

- **Worktrees in the report.** A `git.worktree` row names its main project ("worktree of ~/Projects/app"). When it is not selected, the row says why: the condition it failed (uncommitted changes, ignored content, its own commits, locked, in use, used within the window), "yours: list it to remove it", or that it was moved or copied. A `git.worktree-orphan` row names both causes: its repository is gone, or sits on a volume that is not mounted.
- **What lies inside a worktree is listed under it.** A `.venv` or a `node_modules` inside a worktree is shown indented under the worktree, with its own class, and no longer in its own group. The rows past the first eight are counted on one line, and that line now also counts what lies inside them, so no item drops out of the summary. `--all` lists everything.
- **README: a "Git worktrees" section** says when ddc selects a worktree (the six conditions and the two-day window, `--worktree-min-age`), what happens to one you pick yourself, what `.ddc-keep` does, and what `git worktree repair` is for.
- **Tests.** A worktree whose folder is another one at the same path since the scan is skipped `changed-since-scan`, and no git command runs. A `.ddc-keep` at the top of a worktree keeps it out of the plan.

**Upgrading.** `brew upgrade --cask deepdrivecleaner`, the app's Check Now, the install script, or the zip below. The report schemas do not change: scan 6, clean 10, journal 10, progress 8.
