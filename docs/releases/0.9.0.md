Pick exactly what to clean across every group, and find a coding agent's folders in Finder.

> **DeepDriveCleaner deletes files.** It can remove almost anything it is told to, by you or by an agent acting for you. Use it at your own risk: nobody who made it is responsible for what it removes. Read [DISCLAIMER.md](https://github.com/SmartAndPoint/DeepDriveCleaner/blob/v0.9.0/DISCLAIMER.md).

- **Deselect All in the Review window.** The button sits beside the selection count. It clears every row in every group, rows the filter hides included, and changes nothing else. Then tick what you want in each group and press **Clean**: only the ticked rows go. Scan Again and Settings keep your selection; opening the window again from the popover starts from the default one.
- **Show in Finder for coding agents.** In the Whole disk view, the Coding Agents section's context menu shows a harness's folders or one project's folder in Finder. Sessions, records and transcripts have no menu: they are not folders of their own.

**Upgrading.** From 0.8.0 the app offers this version itself: the popover says "Version 0.9.0 is available", and **Update and Relaunch** installs it. Settings → General → **Check Now** checks at once. `brew upgrade --cask deepdrivecleaner` and the zip below work too.

How it was built, decision by decision, is public too: [DeepDriveCleaner-projectstore](https://github.com/SmartAndPoint/DeepDriveCleaner-projectstore).
