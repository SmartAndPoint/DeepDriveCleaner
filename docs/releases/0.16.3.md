> **DeepDriveCleaner deletes files.** It can remove almost anything it is told to, by you or by an agent acting for you. Use it at your own risk: nobody who made it is responsible for what it removes. Read [DISCLAIMER.md](https://github.com/SmartAndPoint/DeepDriveCleaner/blob/v0.16.3/DISCLAIMER.md).

- **AGPL-3.0-only.** The product (the Rust crates, the CLI, the menu bar app, the scripts) moves from PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0 to the GNU Affero General Public License 3.0. You may use it personally or at work, change it and share it under the AGPL, and ship the unchanged `ddc` beside your own product and call it as a separate program. To build a closed product on its code, ask for a commercial licence: legal@smartandpoint.com.
- **MIT for what others copy.** `agents/**` (the Claude Code skill, the `AGENTS.md` snippet) and `docs/*.schema.json` (every report schema) are under the MIT license, so any project can copy them. Each guidance file carries its own notice.
- **Earlier releases** (0.15.2 and before) were published under PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0 and stay under it.
- The app's About panel, `DISCLAIMER.md` (AGPL sections 7(a), 15 and 16) and `TRADEMARKS.md` (section 7(e): the name and the icon stay SmartAndPoint's) follow. A structure test keeps the licences on their paths. The decision is the vault's ADR "Licensing: AGPL-3.0 with a commercial licence for the product, MIT for agent guidance and report schemas".

- **Windows, first step.** The Windows port of the scanner starts (story "Windows scanner and rule catalog"): the CLI finds its home from `USERPROFILE` and keeps its data in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\DeepDriveCleaner`. Windows is still unsupported: `ddc` there runs only `--version`, `--help` and `rules`.
- **0.16.1 and 0.16.2.** The check that the app's About panel names the AGPL is a DDCKit test: a Rust test, and a script test, may not read the app's files, which a commit can change without running the Rust tiers.

**Upgrading.** `brew upgrade --cask deepdrivecleaner`, the app's Check Now, or the zip below. Nothing in the app or the CLI changed besides the licence texts.
