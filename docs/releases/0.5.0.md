The Trash gets its own row, the app says when the saved report is not of your folders, and the Whole disk view can show its folders in Finder.

> **DeepDriveCleaner deletes files.** It can remove almost anything it is told to, by you or by an agent acting for you. Use it at your own risk: nobody who made it is responsible for what it removes. Read [DISCLAIMER.md](https://github.com/SmartAndPoint/DeepDriveCleaner/blob/v0.5.0/DISCLAIMER.md).

- **The Trash is its own row.** It is no longer counted in Your data. In the Review window the Trash entry says what it is and has **Open the Trash**, which opens it in Finder. There is nothing to select there: you empty the Trash in Finder. After a clean, the result says how much went to the Trash, and the note about APFS space shows only when space was actually freed.
- **The app says when the saved report is not of your folders.** A `ddc scan` of other folders in a terminal writes to the same report the app reads. The Review window and the popover now say so: "This report is of /private, not of your home folder and the folders in Settings", or "This report is of ~/Projects only". **Scan Now** replaces it. When the report is of folders outside your home folder and the folders in Settings, or of another home folder, Clean, Clean selected… and Protect are off. `ddc scan <paths>` into the app's data folder now warns that the app will show this report until its next scan.
- **Show in Finder on Whole disk rows.** Rows that DeepDriveCleaner does not clean itself, such as Applications, Library, /opt, Homebrew, conda and the crash dumps, have a **Show in Finder** button and a context menu. Finder opens at the folders that exist now, at their usual paths, never under /System/Volumes/Data. The macOS updates row has **Open Software Update**. Deleting from these folders is done in Finder, which asks for an administrator's password where one is needed.
- **For scripts.** Each area in `ddc disk --json` carries an optional `reveal` (`finder` or `software-update`). The disk report's version stays 1: the field is new and optional. See [`docs/disk.schema.json`](https://github.com/SmartAndPoint/DeepDriveCleaner/blob/v0.5.0/docs/disk.schema.json).

**Upgrading.** Run `brew upgrade --cask deepdrivecleaner`. The upgrade quits the app. It opens again at your next login if Open at login is on, or when you open it yourself. Do not upgrade while the app is cleaning.
