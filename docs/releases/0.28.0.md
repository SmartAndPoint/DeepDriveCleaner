The menu bar app now notices when used space on your disk grew since the last scan: the icon shows its badge, and the popover says how much, with Scan Now. The terminal gets the same check as `ddc space`. 0.27.0 and 0.26.3 were not released on their own; this release carries them.

> **DeepDriveCleaner deletes files.** It can remove almost anything it is told to, by you or by an agent acting for you. Use it at your own risk: nobody who made it is responsible for what it removes. Read [DISCLAIMER.md](https://github.com/SmartAndPoint/DeepDriveCleaner/blob/v0.28.0/DISCLAIMER.md).

- **"↑ 198 GiB more in use on the startup disk than at the last scan."** The app reads the disk's free space (one reading, no scan) every hour, on wake, when you open the popover, and at most every ten minutes while it runs, and compares it with what the last scan recorded. When used space grew by at least 20 GiB, or 5 % of the disk if that is more, the menu bar icon shows its badge once for that scan (if "Show a badge on the menu bar icon" is on), and the popover shows one line with "A scan can tell where." and Scan Now. Opening the popover clears the badge; a new scan starts the comparison again. Nothing is scanned, notified or removed by itself.
- **A disk nearly full.** Under 10 % free the popover says how full the disk is ("The startup disk is 92% full."), with the growth and Scan Now when it also grew, even before a first scan. `notify_low_disk: false` in `settings.json` turns this off, as it does the notification.
- **`ddc space [--json]`.** The same check in a terminal: "198 GiB more in use on the startup disk than at the last scan, 26 h ago. `ddc scan` shows where." It walks no folder, takes no lock and changes nothing but its own samples (`space.json` in the data folder: one reading an hour, kept for 14 days). `--json` follows [`docs/space.schema.json`](https://github.com/SmartAndPoint/DeepDriveCleaner/blob/v0.28.0/docs/space.schema.json). After the app's clean, the comparison starts from the free space that clean read (`read_at`, a new optional field of the scan report's volumes).
- **What it counts.** The free space is the one `df` and the scan read: purgeable space and local snapshots count as in use, so it can be lower than the Finder's "Available".
- **Linux: btrfs subvolumes are counted, snapshots never are (0.27.0).** On a btrfs home a plain subvolume inside a scan root is now entered and counted; a btrfs snapshot (Snapper, Timeshift, `btrfs subvolume snapshot`) or a `btrfs receive` backup is listed with the reason `snapshot` and never counted or cleaned; a finding that holds a subvolume is never removed (`holds-subvolume`). `ddc du` counts subvolumes below a root.
- **Windows: the in-use check reads your processes (0.26.3).** Windows is still not supported, so nothing of this runs outside the tests yet.

**Report schemas.** The scan report is at 7 since 0.27.0 (`snapshot`, `holds-subvolume`) and gains the optional `read_at`; the space report starts at 1. Clean 10, journal 10, progress 8 are unchanged. Update the app and the CLI together.

**Upgrading.** `brew upgrade --cask deepdrivecleaner`, the app's Check Now, the install script, or the zip below.
