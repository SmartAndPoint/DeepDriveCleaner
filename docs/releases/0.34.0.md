A new command, `ddc changes`, says where your disk space went between the last two scans: by group, elsewhere in your home folder, outside it, and what you cleaned yourself.

> **DeepDriveCleaner deletes files.** It can remove almost anything it is told to, by you or by an agent acting for you. Use it at your own risk: nobody who made it is responsible for what it removes. Read [DISCLAIMER.md](https://github.com/SmartAndPoint/DeepDriveCleaner/blob/v0.34.0/DISCLAIMER.md).

- **`ddc changes`.** It compares `last-scan.json` with `previous-scan.json` (kept since 0.31) and reads the action journal. It prints how much more is in use on your home folder's disk than at the earlier scan, one line per Review group that grew ("Build outputs +186 GiB, 14 new, 9 in Claude Code worktrees or data"), growth elsewhere in your home folder, what is left of the disk's growth outside it with the kinds of folders the scan did not read, and what you cleaned between the two scans. Then every finding that changed, biggest first. `--json` lists everything, schema `docs/changes.schema.json`.
- **Only what both scans could see.** A folder read for the first time (Downloads after you gave Full Disk Access), a folder you stopped skipping, or a rule new in this version is "not compared", never new growth. A `target/` deleted and built again at the same path is one row; a renamed worktree folder is one "moved" row.
- **Your cleans apart.** An item you deleted or trashed through ddc between the two scans is "cleaned by you", not "gone", and the Trash's growth from it is not counted as growth. A `brew cleanup` from the app counts too.
- **When it cannot compare,** it says why in one sentence and exits 0: no scan yet, no earlier one, a report saved before 0.31, a stopped scan, scans of different folders. Growth then shows after the next scan. It takes no lock and writes nothing.

**Report schemas.** New: changes 1. Scan 8, space 1, clean 10, journal 11, progress 8 are unchanged.

**Upgrading.** `brew upgrade --cask deepdrivecleaner`, the app's Check Now, the install script, or the zip below.
