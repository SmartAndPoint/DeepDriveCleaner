A popover that opens ready to use, one group at a time in Review, and an app that keeps to the Mac's conventions.

> **DeepDriveCleaner deletes files.** It can remove almost anything it is told to, by you or by an agent acting for you. Use it at your own risk: nobody who made it is responsible for what it removes. Read [DISCLAIMER.md](https://github.com/SmartAndPoint/DeepDriveCleaner/blob/v0.2.2/DISCLAIMER.md).

- **The popover opens active.** On some Macs it opened looking inactive, with dark glass and grey switches and buttons, until you clicked inside it. It now takes the keyboard as it opens, also when another app was in front.
- **Clean one group from Review.** A group's Select all row says how many of its items are selected and how much they hold, and its Clean N from <group>… button opens the confirmation with that group's selected items only. What you selected in other groups stays selected for later.
- **The keyboard does what a Mac window does.** In Review, Esc clears the filter first and closes the window after; Return does not start a clean while you type in the filter; ⌘F jumps to the filter. While a clean runs, Esc stops it.
- **Mac conventions.** The app follows the accent colour you chose in System Settings. The menus gained Services, Hide Others, Show All, Find, Zoom, Bring All to Front and Help. Buttons and menu items are in title case, the next scan time follows your 12- or 24-hour clock, the Clean button of the confirmation is red, warnings read well in Dark Mode, and the menu bar icon stands still while it works when Reduce Motion is on.

**Upgrading.** `brew upgrade --cask deepdrivecleaner`. The upgrade quits the app; it opens again at your next login if Open at login is on, or when you open it. Do not upgrade while the app is cleaning.

How it was built, decision by decision, is public too: [DeepDriveCleaner-projectstore](https://github.com/SmartAndPoint/DeepDriveCleaner-projectstore).
